VPN Troubleshooting Guide
Employees must connect to the company VPN when accessing internal services from outside 
the office.
If the VPN does not connect, first verify your internet connection.
Restart the VPN client and try again.
Make sure your company credentials are correct.
If the VPN shows error VPN-403, restart the VPN client and reconnect.
If the problem continues, contact IT support and provide the VPN error code.
